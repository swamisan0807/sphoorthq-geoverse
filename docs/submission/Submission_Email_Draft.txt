Proposed solution
SphoorthiQ is a working application that segments real radar (SAR) images of vehicles. It formulates each image as a Markov Random Field, converts it exactly into a QUBO, and solves it two ways on the same objective: an exact classical graph cut and QAOA on Classiq. The two results are compared side by side in a web interface. Real MSTAR chips have no per-pixel masks, so results are also scored against the SARBake reference masks, which are derived from CAD models of the vehicles; the document states what that reference can and cannot show.

Where to find each item in the attached document

Please note that all quantum results to date were obtained on Classiq simulators, not physical quantum hardware. The document states its limitations openly, including where QAOA falls short of the classical optimum and where the brief's worked examples are only partly reproduced.
